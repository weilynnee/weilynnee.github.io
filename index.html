Service

R&D consultation for food and drink brands

Most brands hire an agency for the story and a factory for the product. I work on the part in between, so the product delivers what the brand promises.

Formulation

New recipes from a brief, or reformulation for less sugar, less salt, a shorter ingredient list or a lower cost.

Shelf life and stability

Water activity, pH, packaging and storage trials, so the date on the pack is one you can stand behind.

Sensory testing

Triangle tests and consumer panels that show whether people can taste the change, and whether they like it.

Scale-up

Taking a kitchen recipe to a pilot batch and on to a co-manufacturer's line while keeping what made it good.

Costing

Ingredient and process cost per unit, with options when the margin does not work.

Perceived value

Name, format, portion and pack decisions that change how a product is judged before it is tasted.
